# Ground News — a Claim Checker audit, presented as a StoryTeller story

A worked example of [Claim Checker](https://claimchecker.teambotics.app), the open
method for checking public claims with an AI agent, run by TeamBotics on
**Ground News** — and presented the way every Claim Checker audit is presented:
as a [StoryTeller](https://storyteller.teambotics.app/) story.

**Read it:** open [`index.html`](index.html) — a self-contained story graph of the
audit: 16 claims, their verdicts, and the evidence behind each. Corrected tally:
Confirmed 3 · Partially corroborated 4 · Self-reported only 4 · Unverifiable by
design 2 · Contradicted 3.

**The record behind the story:**

- `GROUND-NEWS-AUDIT-2026-11-13.md` — the audit ledger: 16 claim atoms, the
  seven tests, the contradiction pass, and the validator errata.
- `independent-evidence-ledger.md` — the independent-sources evidence line.
- `VALIDATOR-A-FILING-GN01-08.md`, `VALIDATOR-B-FILING-GN09-16.md` — the two
  independent validator filings ("Cleared with exceptions").
- `ROUND-2-NEXT-QUESTIONS.md` — the ranked follow-up questions the audit earned.
- `research.md` — the story's own research record; `story.json` and `theme.json`
  are the story's source files.

The method: [Claim Checker](https://github.com/Teambotics-Inc/claim-checker)
(Standard v1.0). The presentation companion:
[StoryTeller](https://storyteller.teambotics.app/).

*An independent StoryTeller story by Claim Checker / TeamBotics. Ground News did
not commission, review, or endorse this audit.*
